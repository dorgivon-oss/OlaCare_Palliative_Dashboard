OlaCare
AI-powered system proven to reduce 90% of falls, monitoring resident behavior and streamlining caregiver documentation. Software-only, running on existing cameras in both facilities and home care.
▾

What it does: OlaCare is a software-only solution that prevents falls, monitors nighttime activity, streamlines documentation, and lowers cost per bed. Using standard cameras, OlaCare's Contour Mode delivers 100% resident privacy with no video — fully HIPAA compliant. Backed by strong IP, proprietary computer vision, AI, and domain-specific VIM & VLM. The company founder holds 50+ personal patents, reflecting deep technological capability and a strong foundation for generating additional IP for OlaCare.

Key capabilities: Pre-fall detection and 90% fall prevention · Fall detection with instant alerts · Violence and aggressive behavior detection · 24/7 nighttime activity monitoring (diaper changes, repositioning, wandering) · Hygiene monitoring · Streamlined caregiver documentation (AI pre-fills logs from observed care events, nurse reviews and approves — ~33% admin time reduction). OlaCare also analyzes audio for calls for help, screaming, and signs of distress.

Privacy: OlaCare presents all events through a privacy-preserving contour interface — no video whatsoever. No raw video is stored. All processing is via anonymized metadata. Fully HIPAA compliant.

What it costs: Hardware agnostic — software license per camera. Partners fund hardware. OlaCare provides only the AI intelligence layer, a pure SaaS model with ~85% gross margin.

ROI: Average annual cost per bed in US senior care: $85,400. OlaCare projected annual savings: $42,758 per bed ($31,125 from 75% falls reduction, $11,633 from 33% paperwork reduction). Cost savings far exceed the subscription fee — immediate ROI.

Target market: Assisted living facilities, nursing homes, memory care communities, and home care providers. A pure SaaS model with partner-led distribution — including medical suppliers, digital health platforms, and telecom/broadband providers who bundle OlaCare into their offerings.

SafelyYou
Installs a dedicated proprietary camera in each resident room for fall detection. Saves video clips of events. Offers clinical consulting. Primarily memory care, US/Canada.
▾

What it does: SafelyYou installs its own dedicated camera ("Guardian") in each resident's room. AI detects falls and saves a short video clip for review. Provides clinical consulting — RNs and OTs review fall incidents with staff in "Fall Huddle" sessions.

What it does NOT do: Does not prevent falls (no pre-fall detection). Does not streamline caregiver documentation. Does not detect violence or aggressive behavior. Does not monitor nighttime wandering. Does not track hygiene. Does not work on existing cameras — requires its own dedicated hardware per room. Does not serve the home care market.

What it costs: Dedicated camera per room + professional installation + monthly subscription + clinical services. Estimated $150–300/room/month.

Privacy: Stores actual video clips of real people during fall events.

Coverage: One camera per room. No hallways, common areas, or dining rooms.

Scale: 800+ communities, ~$70M raised. US and Canada only.

2
Vayyar Care
A radar sensor ($250+) per room that detects falls without a camera. Works in darkness and steam. Has no visual understanding — cannot identify who fell, what caused it, or distinguish caregiver from resident.
▾

What it does: Vayyar sells a radar sensor that mounts on the wall/ceiling of each room. Uses radio waves to detect falls, track bed occupancy, count bathroom visits, and monitor room presence. Works in total darkness and through bathroom steam.

What it does NOT do: Does not prevent falls. Does not streamline documentation. Does not detect violence. Does not monitor hygiene. Has zero visual understanding — cannot see what caused a fall, cannot distinguish a caregiver bending down from a resident falling, cannot understand any interaction or situation. Alert says "fall in Room 12" with no context for what happened. Does not serve the home care market at scale.

What it costs: ~$250 per sensor, one or more per room, plus monthly subscription + platform fee + setup. 100 beds = $25,000+ in sensor hardware alone, before recurring fees.

Coverage: One sensor (or more) per room. No hallway or common area coverage.

Scale: Part of Vayyar Imaging ($300M+ raised across all divisions). Partners: TekTone, K4Connect, Amazon Alexa.

3
Nobi
A smart ceiling lamp ($2,288+) with embedded camera. Detects falls and monitors sleep. Requires a licensed electrician for every room. No proven fall reduction data published.
▾

What it does: A ceiling lamp with embedded camera and AI that replaces the room's existing light fixture. Detects falls (asks "Are you OK?" via speaker), tracks bed exits and sleep patterns. Also includes circadian lighting.

What it does NOT do: No published data on fall reduction rates. Does not streamline documentation. Does not detect violence. Does not monitor hygiene. Cannot work on existing cameras — each room requires purchasing a proprietary lamp and hiring an electrician. Not viable for large-scale home care deployment due to cost per unit.

What it costs: $2,288+ per lamp. Requires licensed electrician for installation in each room. For a 100-bed facility: $228,800+ in hardware alone, plus electrician labor for every room, plus ongoing subscription.

Coverage: One lamp per room only. No hallways or common areas.

Scale: Belgian company, expanding into US market.

4
Securitas Foresite
Multi-sensor system: 3D depth camera + motion sensors + bed sheet sensor — three hardware types per room. Good fall detection, predictive risk scoring. Enterprise-only, long sales cycles.
▾

What it does: Installs three sensor types per room: (1) 3D depth sensor, (2) motion sensors, (3) bed sensor (fitted sheet). AI trained on 20+ years of movement data predicts fall risk and flags early health indicators (UTI, pneumonia, heart failure).

What it does NOT do: Does not streamline documentation. Does not detect violence. Does not monitor hygiene. Cannot work on existing cameras. 300+ individual devices per 100 beds, plus bed sheets that wear out. Facility-only — no home care application. Enterprise-only through authorized partners — long procurement cycles.

What it costs: Not public. Enterprise pricing. 3+ devices per room = 300+ devices for 100 beds. Long-term contracts.

Coverage: One sensor set per room. No hallways or common areas.

Scale: Division of Securitas Group (public).

5
Sensi.AI
Audio-only monitoring via a plug-and-play device. Listens to sounds and speech 24/7 to detect health changes. Home care only. No visual capability — silent falls are invisible. $98M+ raised.
▾

What it does: A plug-and-play audio device placed in a senior's home. Continuously records and analyzes all environmental sounds and speech. AI detects changes in physical health, emotional state, and cognitive function. Can identify emotional and cognitive changes through voice tone and speech patterns.

What it does NOT do: Cannot see anything — audio only. Cannot detect silent falls (extremely common in elderly). No visual fall prevention. No streamlined caregiver documentation. No violence detection through vision. No hygiene monitoring. No visual feedback of any kind. Home care only — does not serve facilities.

Privacy concern: The system continuously records all audio in the home — every conversation, every family visit, every interaction. This may create significant discomfort for families and residents who are aware that everything said in the home is being captured and analyzed.

What it costs: B2B SaaS subscription for home care agencies. $98M+ raised.

Key partners: Visiting Angels, Caring Senior Service, Griswold Home Care, Assistance Home Care, Teepa Snow's Positive Approach to Care® (PAC).
